# Historical Research & Fact-check Guardrails

### 1206
- Chinggis Khan founded Great Mongol State (Их Монгол Улс) in 1206. Source: https://mongoltoli.mn/history/h/504
- Great Khural / Хуралдай as historical institution (not a modern representative legislature): https://mongoltoli.mn/history/h/503
- Great Yassa / Их засаг: full original text has not survived. Don't invent clauses or pretend it is the 1924 constitution: https://mongoltoli.mn/history/h/1004

### 1911
- 1911 Dec 29: Bogd Khan enthroned, independence restored: https://mongoltoli.mn/history/h/665

### 1921
- 1921 national democratic revolution: https://mongoltoli.mn/history/h/643
- IMPORTANT: monarchy was not instantly abolished in 1921; Bogd Khan remained head of state before his death in May 1924.

### 1924
- 1924 Nov 26, 16:17: first constitution passed; resolution no.14, article 1; 6 chapters, 50 articles. Official Parliament: https://old.parliament.mn/nn/63426/
- 77 delegates attended (of 90 selected), 18 pages original manuscript, 72-page explanation. https://old.parliament.mn/nn/63426/
- Government historian article dates first Khural opening 1924 Nov 8: https://www.sgsa.gov.mn/pbuilding/story/1207050001/detail
- Historical dictionary also says 1924 Nov 8: https://mongoltoli.mn/history/h/701
- **Contradiction:** Other institutional articles say 1924 Sep 8, e.g. Parliament 2024 article and National History Museum 2020 article. Avoid giving an opening *date* in narration because sources conflict: https://old.parliament.mn/nn/63426/ and https://www.nhm.gov.mn/client/mn/article/109
- The constitutional first article declares a sovereign people's republic, authority to the people and the Great Khural. https://old.parliament.mn/nn/63426/
- Article 34 introduced voting/eligibility for men and women age 18+ **under specific occupational criteria**, and Article 35 excluded certain groups incl. former nobles, some clergy, merchants/usurers etc. Avoid modern universal-suffrage claims. Primary document: https://uat.legalinfo.mn/api/front/cons-detail-1924.html
- Soviet advisors participated in drafting; do not depict independence as an unqualified absence of foreign influence. https://mongoltoli.mn/history/h/701
- 1924 was not the introduction of modern pluralist democracy; 1992 constitution marked a distinct later change.
- Constitution changed 1940, 1960, 1992. https://old.parliament.mn/nn/13466/

### Presentation ethics / authenticity
- Distinguish artwork, reenactment, photo and archival document.
- Do not invent the precise appearance or conversation of historical actors.
- Dates are facts; visual symbols are metaphors. Never ascribe fictional direct quotations to historical figures.
- For history class source citations must be in end credits or a PDF/MD bibliography.

### Republic Day
Nov 26 is observed as Republic Day / Улс тунхагласны өдөр, connected to the 1924 first constitution and proclamation; https://montsame.mn/en/article/mpost-356132
