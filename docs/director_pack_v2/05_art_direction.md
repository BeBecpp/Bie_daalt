# Art Direction Bible

**Film pitch:** History in eight cinematic movements — from the Khan's Great Khural to the 1924 First Constitution; time represented as the evolving idea of state authority, not a single line of 'identical' states.

## Visual system
- Master: 1920×1080 / 16:9 / 24fps; cinematic letterbox optional but keep title-safe within 10%.
- Palette: charcoal `#11110F`, parchment ivory `#E8D6B7`, oxidized copper `#A46A3A`, antique gold `#C8A56C`, muted crimson `#782E2F`.
- Period progression: 1206 gold dawn → 1911 smoky sepia → 1921 cool/desaturated → 1924 parchment + deep red → modern neutral daylight.
- Typography: Noto Serif (headlines), Noto Sans (subtitles) or free SIL OFL equivalent. Modern subtitles must not impersonate original text.
- Camera: 35mm for landscapes, 50–85mm simulated for manuscript macro shots. Slow dolly, deep focus for maps, shallow DOF for artifacts. No random camera jitters.
- Geometry: low-poly/designed rather than uncanny photorealistic humans.
- On-screen captions: 2–4 seconds hold, max 10 words; dates large and consistent.
- Use animated parallax on scanned photos and proper archival attribution. Do not colorize photos in a way that suggests original color certainty.

## Audio
- Narrator: calm, grounded, neither overly patriotic nor robotic; Mongolian accent, medium slow.
- SFX: clock 16:17, hoof beats, wind, cloth, page-turn, wooden furniture, ink stamp (symbolic only).
- Music: original or license-verified CC0/public domain track; Mongolian strings/morin-khuur motifs if licensed. Never copy a film soundtrack.
- Keep voice intelligible, lower background music when speech plays; final silence/room ambience ~4 seconds.
- Subtitles: Mongolian, safe area, 1–2 lines, accurate orthography.

## Scene warnings
1. The 'Mongol Empire' borders aren't interchangeable with modern Mongolia's territorial borders.
2. `Их засаг` text survives only fragmentarily; avoid exact 'scroll of law' invented script.
3. The Great Khural and a modern elected parliament are NOT institutionally equivalent.
4. 1924 document in classical Mongolian script; use actual scan or a clearly labeled abstract depiction.
5. Period correct flags or avoid flags entirely where exact reconstruction cannot be sourced.

## Deliverables and acceptance
- 8 scene sequences, assembled and sync'd, mixed audio;
- main MP4 1080p H.264/AAC; SRT subtitles;
- project sources Blender .blend/bpy, Kdenlive .kdenlive, audio, asset manifest;
- closing source card (4–6 seconds);
- no unlicensed archival images/music in final output.
