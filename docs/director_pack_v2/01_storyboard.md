## Visual continuity
- Geometric match cuts, never cheesy spin/wipes.
- Historical period 1206: sculpted silhouettes, broad-angle cinematography; avoid confident face reconstruction.
- Years 1911–1924: period-appropriate garments, flags, manuscripts. Never show today's flag as authentic 1924 flag.
- All 1924 archive clips are stills, not claimed to be moving historical footage.
- Do not render Cyrillic writing on a purported original 1924 manuscript; use historical Mongolian script source image, overlays may be modern Cyrillic subtitles.
- Use the **same symbolic red-ink/light motif** across shots, but never imply a literal seal survived unchanged across centuries.

## POC acceptance
A 7–10 second Scene 01 preview with real 3D parchment, animated 16:17 watch, moving camera, lights/shadows, readable Mongolian heading, exportable frames.
