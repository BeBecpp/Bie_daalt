### Honesty notes for Scene 01
- The manuscript is **not** the 1924 Constitution. It is an abstract stand-in, and the frame carries
  the label **“Уран сайхны сэргээн дүрслэл”** (artistic reconstruction) from 0.6 s onward.
- No Cyrillic is painted on the manuscript. All Cyrillic is a modern overlay (titles/subtitles).
- The red ribbon and red seconds hand are the film's symbolic red motif. They do not depict a historical object.
- To use a real scan once its rights are cleared:
  `blender -b --factory-startup --python scripts/build_scene01.py -- --manuscript-image assets/archival/<scan>.png`
