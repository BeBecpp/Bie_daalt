# CLAUDE.md — Mandatory Project Rules
You are the technical director, procedural 3D animator, motion graphics developer, and QC agent for a Grade 10 Mongolian history short documentary.

## Source of truth
Read in order: `README.md`, `01_storyboard.md`, `02_narration_mn.md`, `03_fact_check_sources.md`, `04_pipeline.md`, `05_art_direction.md`, `06_asset_credits.md`, and `shots.json`.

## Objective
Create a fully runnable, open-source-first Blender + FFmpeg project with 8 shots for a 4 minute 1080p cinematic documentary: `16:17 — Монгол төрийн түүхэн эргэлт`. Use real, moving 3D camera, geometry, light, scripted animation, and 2.5D layered archival source-safe visuals. Do NOT substitute a static HTML slideshow.

## Required approach
- Write genuine Blender Python (`bpy`) scripts; organize reusable helpers; commit/keep sources, not just video outputs.
- Build a small Scene 01 proof of concept FIRST: 7–10s, parchment under light, camera dolly, clock 16:17, dust particles, filmic highlights; export preview and inspect generated PNGs. No use of external image generator or proprietary effects.
- After POC, implement all scenes as reusable scripted scene builders plus `render_all` shell commands or Python orchestration. Render sample frames for every scene and check visually before full render.
- Make every shot distinctive with purposeful motion; prefer cinematography, layered parallax, animation and historical grounding to realism of faces.
- Use legitimate sourced archival materials only if their usage rights are verified; when absent use 3D procedural reconstructions and leave clearly named placeholders. Keep an asset license manifest.
- No invented quotes, no claim the Great Yassa survives intact, no claim 1206 and 1924 are identical states, no wrong contemporary flags in 1924, no modern universal franchise in 1924, no fake archive video.
- Subtitles and title must support Cyrillic Mongolian with properly bundled, licensed fonts, no broken glyphs.
- Scene timings must allow time stretching to actual narration generated by ElevenLabs.
- Existing Python scripts and assets must be editable, and all scripts must have clear run instructions.
- Keep rendering performant for student laptops: Eevee, simplified scene geometry, 540p previews, per-scene final renders, no full 4-min Blender render if slow.
- If an external resource cannot be fetched, keep working with geometry and placeholders and clearly record that limitation. Don't invent download URLs.
- Deliver an MP4 project export command and verification checklist, plus editable .blend sources. If actual render is not available, give precise instructions and state exactly what's incomplete.

## Historical non-negotiables
1924 Nov 26 16:17 is verified; six chapters/fifty articles; 77 delegates; first written Constitution; monarchy formally replaced by republic. Article 34 and Article 35 differ: avoid claiming universal voting for all. First Great Khural's opening day conflicting across institutional sources, so avoid specific opening date. `Их засаг` manuscript not extant in full.

## Quality bar
The project must show evidence of actual camera motion, dynamic light, shadows, clear 3D separation or multi-plane depth, adequate subtitle contrast, no copyright-infringing soundtrack, and Mongolian narration that has been fact-checked.

## Implementation notes (added by the technical director)
- The director pack is in `docs/director_pack/`; read it in the order listed above (paths are relative to that folder).
- Builders: `scripts/build_sceneNN.py` use `scripts/lib/` (`common`, `materials`, `props`, `textures`, `overlay`). All take the same CLI; beats are expressed relative to `--duration` so shots can be stretched to the narration WAV.
- Titles/subtitles are rendered in a separate orthographic "Titles" scene and composited in, so they never get camera DOF and are clearly modern overlays. Every reconstruction shot shows the label "Уран сайхны сэргээн дүрслэл".
- Thin glass must use `materials.glass()` (transparent + fresnel, front faces only). A refractive or solidified crystal turns the watch dial black in Cycles when caustics are off (found during POC review).
- Before a full render, always render `--stills` and inspect them. Log findings in `docs/QC_CHECKLIST.md`.
- Keep `ASSET_MANIFEST.md` current. Do not embed any archival file whose rights are unverified.
- Director pack v2 lives in `docs/director_pack_v2/`; approved archival assets are listed in `assets/archival/manifest.json` and read by `scripts/lib/archive.py`. `scripts/render_all.sh` skips Scenes 02–04 unless `python3 fetch_commons_assets.py --verify-only` exits 0.
